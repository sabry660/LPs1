MASTER THE BASICS

Hover each card to reveal step-by-step form cues and common mistakes to avoid.

01
Legs · Glutes · Core
SQUAT
↗ Hover to learn form
SQUAT FORM
  1. Bar on upper traps, feet shoulder-width apart
  2. Brace core and take a deep breath
  3. Break at hips and knees simultaneously
  4. Descend until thighs parallel to floor
  5. Drive through heels to stand explosively
⚠ Avoid: Knee cave, butt wink
02
Back · Hamstrings · Glutes
DEAD-LIFT
↗ Hover to learn form
DEADLIFT FORM
  1. Bar over mid-foot, hip-width stance
  2. Hinge at hips, grip just outside legs
  3. Proud chest, neutral spine, lats engaged
  4. Push floor away, keep bar close to body
  5. Lock out hips at top, controlled descent
⚠ Avoid: Rounding lower back
03
Chest · Shoulders · Triceps
BENCH PRESS
↗ Hover to learn form
BENCH FORM
  1. Grip 1.5× shoulder width, retract scapula
  2. Arch naturally, feet flat on floor
  3. Unrack, bar over lower chest
  4. Lower with control, touch chest lightly
  5. Press explosively, fully extend elbows
⚠ Avoid: Flared elbows, bouncing
04
Back · Biceps · Rear Delts
PULL-UP
↗ Hover to learn form
PULL-UP FORM
  1. Overhand grip, slightly wider than shoulders
  2. Dead hang, depress and retract scapula
  3. Pull elbows down and back toward hips
  4. Chin clears bar, squeeze lats at top
  5. Lower fully — no half reps
⚠ Avoid: Kipping, shrugging neck
05
Shoulders · Triceps · Upper Back
OHP
↗ Hover to learn form
OHP FORM
  1. Standing, grip just outside shoulders
  2. Bar resting on front delts, elbows forward
  3. Brace core, glutes squeezed tight
  4. Press bar directly overhead, lock out
  5. Return to collarbone height, repeat
⚠ Avoid: Lower back arch, bar drift
06
Hamstrings · Glutes · Spinal Erectors
ROMANIAN DEADLIFT
↗ Hover to learn form
RDL FORM
  1. Stand tall, bar at hips, soft knee bend
  2. Hinge at hips, push them back behind you
  3. Bar drags down your legs, back stays flat
  4. Feel hamstring stretch, lower to mid-shin
  5. Drive hips forward to return to start
⚠ Avoid: Squatting, rounding back
